5. TOOL TYPES AND OPTIONS
=========================

The same options work as tool types of the program's icon (select the icon,
then Information from the Icons menu) and as Shell arguments. A tool type
in parentheses, such as (LANG=en), is switched off; remove the parentheses
to use it. The icon comes with every option at its default.

  LANG=en | pl | ja
      Language of the text. Default: en. LANG=pl plays the Polish
      translation. LANG=ja plays the original Japanese text; no Japanese
      system software is needed, the game brings its own font.

  MODE=<screen mode id>
      The screen mode, as a hexadecimal mode id. Default: the best mode
      AROS finds for 640x400 with 256 colours. Any mode of at least
      640x400 works; a larger one shows the picture in its middle.

  AUDIO=ahi | none
      Sound output. Default: ahi. "none" plays silently.

  AHIUNIT=<n>
      The AHI unit to use. Default: 0.

  RATE=<Hz>
      The sample rate of the music and sound. Default: 44100.

  AUDIOCPU=<percent>
      How much of the processor the sound may use, from 10 to 100.
      Default: 75. If the sound needs more, short gaps of silence are
      inserted instead, so that the game and the system stay responsive.

  AUDIOBUF=<samples>
      Size of each sound buffer. Default: 4096 at 44100 Hz, about a tenth
      of a second. Larger buffers survive heavy disk activity better but
      delay sound effects.

  VOICEPAK=<file>
      Where the voice data is (section 3, "Voices"). Default:
      xenon_voice.pak in the program's drawer, or next to the PAK= file.
      The characters speak whenever this file is found.

  NOVOICE
      No voices, even when the voice data is there.

  VOICEVOL=<percent>
      Volume of the voices, 1 to 100. Default: 100.

  MUSICVOL=<percent>
      Volume of the music and the sound effects, 1 to 100. Default: 50
      while the voices play (the music would cover them), 100 without
      them.

  VOICESTRICT
      A click during a voice does not cut it short: the line waits until
      the voice has finished, as in the Windows version. Default: off (OK
      or a click cuts the voice; Cancel always does).

  VOICEMIX
      With AUDIO=ahi: the game mixes the voices into the music itself
      instead of playing them on a second AHI channel. Needed when the AHI
      unit is set to a single channel in the AHI preferences (the voices
      are silent otherwise). The voices then play at the music's rate.

  OPN=lite | lite2
      The FM sound emulation. Default: lite. lite2 renders the FM part
      at double rate for a cleaner sound.

  UPLOAD=chunky | rtg
      How the picture is drawn. Default: chunky. Leave it unless the
      picture looks wrong with your graphics driver.

  WINDOW
      Play in a window on the desktop instead of on the game's own
      screen. Default: off. The screen needs 256 colours or a graphics
      card (16 free colours for the game) and room for 640x400 pixels. If
      the screen cannot take the window, the game opens its own screen
      as usual. In a window F10 changes its size (section 4).

  PUBSCREEN=<name>
      With WINDOW: the public screen to open the window on. Default: the
      default public screen, normally Wanderer's.

  SCALE=1 | 2 | 3 | auto
      Show each pixel of the game 1, 2 or 3 times as large. Default: 1.
      "auto" takes the largest that fits. On the game's own screen the
      game picks a mode large enough (MODE= insists on one mode).

  ASPECT
      Show the picture with the proportions of the PC-98's 4:3 monitor,
      by repeating every fifth line (640x400 becomes 640x480). Default:
      off.

  PRELOAD=auto | yes | no
      Load all game data into memory at start-up. Default: auto, which
      preloads when the data fits with 1.5 MB to spare. "yes" preloads
      whenever it fits, "no" never preloads.

  PAK=<file>
      Where the data file is. Default: xenon.pak in the program's drawer.

  QUIET
      No messages in the Shell window.

The program also understands AUTOPLAY, MAXTICKS, FAST, SNAPDIR and
PRESENTTEST. They exist for automated testing and are of no use for
playing.
