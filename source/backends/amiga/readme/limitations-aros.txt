6. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
====================

  - Tested in a virtual machine and hosted on Linux only, not on a real
    PC. Reports from AROS users are welcome.
  - The screen is not synchronised with the display's refresh: a page
    switch can tear on some graphics drivers.
  - Save files of the PC-98 version cannot be imported.
  - The game does not pause when you switch to Wanderer.
  - A window (WINDOW) has no double buffering: a new picture may tear.
