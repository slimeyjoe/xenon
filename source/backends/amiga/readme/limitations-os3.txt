6. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
====================

  - On a 68040 at 25 MHz the music plays at 11 kHz. Faster rates need a
    faster processor.
  - Graphics card support has been tested in an emulator only.
  - The WarpOS (PowerPC) version has been tested in an emulator only.
  - Save files of the PC-98 version cannot be imported.
  - The game does not pause when you switch to the Workbench.
  - In a window (WINDOW) the game needs a Workbench of 256 colours or a
    graphics card; with 8 or 16 colours it opens its own screen. A
    window has no double buffering: a new picture may tear, and while
    you drag a window around the picture stops (the music plays on).
