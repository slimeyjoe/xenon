1. WHAT THE GAME IS
===================

Xenon is a Japanese visual novel published by C's Ware in 1994 for the NEC
PC-9801. You read the story page by page and steer it at a few points by
picking from a short menu.

Kouji, a young man, is a paid test subject at a research and medical
centre. A doctor treats his recurring nightmare with hypnosis. In the dream
he is someone else, aboard a spaceship near an alien planet, and he cannot
tell which world is the real one. The choices you make in the prologue send
you into one of four dreams. Each one tells a different story with the same
cast. There are seven endings in all.

This port runs the original game's scripts, pictures and music unchanged.
It plays in English by default, using a complete fan translation. A
Polish translation and the original Japanese text are included as well
(see LANG in section 5). With the voice data of the 1997 Windows version,
made from your own copy (section 3), the characters speak their lines.

The PC-98 version is reproduced faithfully. That includes its timing, its
controls and its odd corners, which section 4 explains.
